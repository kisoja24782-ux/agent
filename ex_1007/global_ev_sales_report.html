Global Electric Vehicle Sales (2016-2025)

Summary Report

This report provides an overview of the global electric vehicle sales from 2016 to 2025. The data shows a remarkable growth in the adoption of electric vehicles worldwide.

Key Highlights

  • 2016: 770,000 vehicles sold
  • 2017: 1,230,000 vehicles sold
  • 2018: 2,030,000 vehicles sold
  • 2019: 2,230,000 vehicles sold
  • 2020: 3,190,000 vehicles sold
  • 2021: 6,630,000 vehicles sold
  • 2022: 10,290,000 vehicles sold
  • 2023: 13,410,000 vehicles sold
  • 2024: 16,460,000 vehicles sold
  • 2025: 20,700,000 vehicles sold

Markets Insights

China leads the market with approximately 62% of global EV sales in 2025. Europe accounts for over 25% of new car sales being electric, while in the US, 10% of new car sales are electric and hybrids.

Future Outlook

It is anticipated that by 2026, global passenger EV sales will reach about 23.3 million, comprising 27% of all new cars. By 2035, over half of all passenger vehicles are expected to be electric.
